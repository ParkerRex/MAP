### What is a Government Food-Subsidy?

There’s a simple rule in economics of government that says if you want more of something, subsidize that thing.

If you want less of something tax that thing. Simple carrot and stick approach. We see how that plays out in our food.

If the farmer grows corn, the government will pay them a certain amount of money per acre. If they grow soy, they’ll get a certain amount of money per acre. If they grow fruits and vegetables, they get nothing.

Farmers are incentivized to grow corn and soy. They are not incentivized to grow fruits and vegetables. That's why when you go to the store it's so much more expensive for you to get organic raspberries than it is for you to get anything that is made from corn or soy.

The government incentivizes farmers to grow crops that poison our population. It encourages farmers to grow as much food as possible. We grow 2x the amount of calories the population needs. 3 crops get 70% of the money, ~$6B a year. Thats corn, wheat and soybean.
