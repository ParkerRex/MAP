Strength in position comes from:

- Metrics
- Revenue growth
- Customer retention
- How many people we’re serving

## VC look for

### #1 Billion $ Potential

- 📈 How large is this market
- 💰 Whats your exit strategy?
- 🧮 How did you calculate your “Total Addressable Market”

### #2 Competitive Differentiation

_is this the one to bet on in the market?_

- 🤔What makes this different from competition
- 🏃 How will you keep up with the industry?
- 🎯 What is your entry “wedge”?

### #3 Quality of the Team

_Why are these people the ones to do it?_

- 🤝 Do you have founder-market-fit?
- 👥 How will you hire the best possible team?
- 👩‍💻 What experience do you have in this field?

Weave all these attributes into the conversation.

Want investors to build sense of conviction in our business.
