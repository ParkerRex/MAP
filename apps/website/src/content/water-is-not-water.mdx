### So whats up with the water? Why should I care?

- Americans drink over 1 billion glasses of [tap water](https://theberkey.com/blogs/water-filter/comparing-tap-water-and-bottled-waters-beneficial-mineral-for-consumers) each day.
- It is a common belief that you have to drink 6-8 glasses of water per day, but this myth comes from a fundamental misunderstanding of some basic physiology. [McGill study disproving](https://www.mcgill.ca/oss/article/health-nutrition/water-myth)
- In the past 12 years, drinking water used by 49 million residents of the United States has been contaminated with bacteria, **radioactive materials,** and arsenic in levels that are both illegal and incredibly unsafe.
- On June 15th, 2022, The U.S. Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) issued an unprecedented and stunning health advisory that affects millions of Americans from coast to coast: **Two notorious chemicals commonly detected in public drinking water supplies, PFOA and PFOS, are far more toxic than previously thought and can pose serious human health risks, including cancer, infertility, low birthweight, thyroid issues, and more, even at undetectable, “near zero” levels.**
- In 2016, a Harvard study found PFASs, another very harmful chemical substance, in water from 33 of the 50 states, and determined that over six million United States residents were affected by these contaminated water supplies.
- Prescription medications have also been found in the tap water of **41,000,000 American homes.**
- According to the Associated Press, there is a vast array of pharmaceuticals including antibiotics, anti-convulsants, mood stabilizers, and sex hormones in the municipal water supplies

  > “At least 45% of the nation’s tap water is estimated to have one or more types of the chemicals known as per- and polyfluorinated alkyl substances, or PFAS, according to a new study by the U.S. Geological Survey. There are more than 12,000 types of PFAS, not all of which can be detected with current tests; the USGS study tested for the presence of 32 types”
